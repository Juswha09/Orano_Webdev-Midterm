Privacy principles

Important limitation

Local storage is not encrypted by this app. Anyone with access to your browser profile may be able to inspect it. This demo does not implement end-to-end encryption, secure authentication, server-side controls, or a guarantee against AI surveillance.

Quick tip

Avoid using real passwords or sensitive personal information in prototypes.
